💡 Key Analytical Insights & Strategic Recommendations

1. Dynamic Buffer Cooking

Transition from static 12% excess buffer to dynamic 4% safety margin using 7-day rolling forecasts. Estimated net savings: ₹9.85 Lakhs / semester.

2. Weekend Prep Downscaling

Breakfast footfall drops by 38% on Saturdays and Sundays. Reducing morning procurement automatically on weekends eliminates ~45 kg of wasted food weekly.

3. Counter Staff Staggering

Queue congestion peaks between 13:00–13:35 and 20:15–20:45. Opening a 2nd verification counter during these windows cuts average wait time from 9.4 min to under 3.5 min.
